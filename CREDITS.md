# Photo credits

The Saanvi Atelier campaign is designed over these photographs, found through [Openverse](https://openverse.org) and [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org). Each one is free for commercial use under the licence shown: CC0 needs no credit, CC BY requires the credit below. Several saris are museum pieces whose designs are long in the public domain.
